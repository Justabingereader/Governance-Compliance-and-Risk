### ADR-003: App Service Workload Selection

Status: Accepted

Supersedes: VM Workload Decision(Oct 2026)

Context: After infrastructural challenges with selecting a VM in the north europe region that were not accessible due to high regional demand (B1s capacity-restricted → Basv2 quota 0 → Bsv2 quota request denied (high demand, on 03/11/2026) → all D2 sizes Location-restricted → confidential families at 0 quota), existing alternatives such as app service plan was considered even though the VM infrastructure code had been developed in bicep. 

Decision: it was decided to go forward with the App service plan as the workload for this project rather than apply for quota increase across VMs that weren't accessible due to high regional demand.

Consequences: Reversal of existing code infrastructure, change to Network Security group rules, change to the operation of existing infrastructure, Loss of VM patching and hardening exercise, change to the architecture of the project. The absence of a VM to patch leaves a reduced attack surface to manage with a lower cost incurred by the project, the lost skills from the patching and hardening exercise would be moved to a different project, an intune lab, and i chose not to take a narrow policy exception for other VMsizes due to the requirement of a quota increase requirement, with a defined time limit incurred by this sidetrack not deterministic.

Revision Trigger: Change to project architecture, or the unavailability of app service plan with the availability of a VMsize that is acceptable by the policy
