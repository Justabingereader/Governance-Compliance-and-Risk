### ADR-004: Workload is a VM, Superseding ADR-003

Status: Accepted

Supersedes: ADR-003

Context: The Bsv2 quota increase was accepted on 04/11/2026, giving 2 vcpu's in north europe region. Support case number of 2611030050004662 with manual verification of quota increase on azure portal. with the revision trigger on ADR-003 satisfying the conditions towards the VM workload decision and ADR-004. 

Decision: Restoration of VM module into the architecture, use of VMsize Standard_B2ls_v2.

Consequences: Patching and hardening exercise comes back, with the absorption of costs that come along with the introduction of the VM, including an increased attack surface. The VM would be deallocated when not in use.

Revision Trigger: if the quota increase for the VM is reversed, or the cost exceeds the €200 monthly budget.
