### ADR-001: No Firewall

Status: Accepted

Context: The use of a premium firewall tier in the hub component of the architecture, inclusion would allow traffic inspection, access control, Inter-Network Security and egress protection. 

Decision: I decided to reject the use of firewall deployment in the architecture due to the €1,140 cost that would be incurred by the deployment. This decision was enforced at the policy level, with a withstanding policy that enforces the prevention of the deployment of a firewall to the infrastructure.

Consequences: The architecture has now accepted the lack of use of Intrusion Detection and prevention systems to detect possible compromise of the infrastructure. The non-transitive nature of spoke-to-spoke peering in the architecture ensures the restriction of east-west traffic flow. Egress protection, access-control and inter-network security features offered by the firewall have also been deemed inaccessible. Microsoft sentinel is to serve as the compensating control for the lack of Intrusion Detection and Prevention Systems, with proactive security control lost, sentinel is to serve as a reactive security control, which is a residual risk that has been accepted in the design of the architecture. A compensating control for the security risk posed by the architecture is the use of defaultouboundaccess: false, removing default communication by subnets to outbound networks.

Revision Trigger: This ADR would only be superseded if an additional €1,140 monthly operating cost is added to the project.
